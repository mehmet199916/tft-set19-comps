Method, scoring model & caveats

Scoring model (tools/score.py)

  • +1.0 per non-unique trait at its first breakpoint (86% of top-20 traits sit there)
  • +1.6 if a carry's own trait is at tier 2+, -1.6 if a level 8-9 board pushes none (Invoker 5.15 → 3.67, Executioner 3.91 → 2.79)
  • +0.15 per extra tier on other traits (pushing non-carry traits: no gain)
  • +1.5 × share of units in 2+ active traits (Mama Beak: 7 traits on 7 units)
  • -1.2 per unit in no active trait, -0.4 per wasted unit past a breakpoint (0.34-place swing)
  • +1.0 opener bonus: 5 units, 4+ traits, ≤11 gold (76-81% round-win)
  • -0.8 per unlockable unit; none allowed in openers
  • Hard limits: 4-5 frontline and 2+ backline damage dealers at level 7-9 (2-4 front early), cost curve per level (7 frontliners: 5.76 avg)

The search scored about 15,900 final boards (beam 300 for 47 forced carries, plus a wide level-5 search), then the shortlist was curated by hand around carries with real damage.

Top-scoring boards that were rejected

    Known model biases

    • Galio and Lucian (cheap 2-trait Jazz glue) appear in most top boards: the model rewards their trait links but cannot judge unit strength.
    • R&B 2 (Mel + Rell) counts as a "pushed" trait although it is a soul-link utility.

    Data notes

    • Beach Pop is listed with breakpoint 0 in the PBE file; MetaTFT's set page and tactics.tools both use 3 / 5 / 7, which is used here.
    • K/DA counts star levels: the script assumes 1-3 costs are 2-star on level 7-9 boards and only 1-costs on level 4-5 boards.
    • Unique 1-unit traits count as active but are listed separately; per the PBE tooltip, Jazz and the Smeech unlock only count non-unique traits. Heartsteel and R&B start at 1 unit.
    • Collab (random each game), Sona (level-10 unlock) and other 7-costs are ignored unless a card says so.
    • Team codes follow MetaTFT's Team Planner format ("02" + three hex characters per unit, cheapest first, padded to 10 + "TFTSet19"). A generated code imported into MetaTFT's team builder loaded the same 8 units. tactics.tools' and MetaTFT's own copy buttons do not produce Set 19 codes yet, and in-game acceptance on PBE is unverified. Graves, Mordekaiser and Nasus share one id in the PBE file, so codes with them are flagged.
    • Board positions are a starting layout matching the tactics.tools link, not optimised positioning.
